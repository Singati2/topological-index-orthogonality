## Interpretation

A row with `fraction min = $P_n$` $= 1.00$ and `fraction max = $S_n$` $= 1.00$ across all $n$ is consistent with Conjecture D.1 in its stated sign region. A fraction strictly between 0 and 1 indicates that the conjecture is order-dependent at that parameter triple --- the extremal switches at some $n$ --- which is interesting information for the formal derivation. A fraction of 0 indicates that the path / star is the *opposite* extremal in that parameter region (e.g. for $\alpha < 0$ the path is typically the maximizer).
